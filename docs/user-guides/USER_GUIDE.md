# User guide

**Getting around.** Use the sidebar or press **Ctrl/Cmd-K** to search any record by code (e.g. `PRJ-0002`) or name. **Create** (top bar) opens quick-create for work items, risks, issues, ideas, change requests, projects, products and decisions. The bell shows notifications (approvals, assignments, reviews due).

**Records.** Every table supports search, status filter, sorting and paging; click a row to open its drawer: *Details* (fields and computed values), *Workflow & approvals* (start/advance lifecycle, submit for approval, approve/reject/delegate) and *History* (audit trail). Statuses that require governance (e.g. a budget “approved”) cannot be selected directly – submit the record instead.

**Command centre.** Grey/hatched means *no data*, never “good”. Each chart has a table icon for an accessible data view. Click bubbles, bars and heatmap cells to drill down.

**Projects.** Run **BIA screening** early – it decides whether a full/light BIA is needed and creates a draft for the BC team. Use *Governance & gates* to move through the lifecycle; unmet criteria are listed. *Status report* generates a draft document that flags any missing information. *Close* never closes the linked products.

**Products.** Products live beyond projects: manage roadmap lanes, discovery (ideas, feedback, experiments, RICE), releases, operations and resilience from the product workspace.

**BIA.** In the impact matrix click (or focus + Enter) a cell to cycle severity. MTPD and tier recalculate on save; findings explain gaps (e.g. RTO exceeds MTPD, dependency slower than RTO). A BIA with blocking findings cannot be submitted.

**Approvals.** *Governance → My approvals*. Rejections need a reason, conditional approvals need conditions. You cannot approve your own financial/procurement/risk/change/BIA requests or amounts above your authority.

**AI assistant.** Ask questions in *AI intelligence*; answers cite records you are allowed to see and show confidence. Suggestions (e.g. risks, actions) are only saved when you accept them. The assistant cannot approve, accept risks, change budgets or deploy.
